# Meeting Notes & Action Items

Use this when the user pastes a transcript, notes, or asks to summarize a discussion.

## Procedure

1. Read the entire input; identify participants and the meeting's purpose.
2. Separate facts/decisions from discussion/suggestions.
3. Extract concrete action items with owners and deadlines if stated.

## Output format

- **Summary** — 2-4 sentences on what the meeting was about and its outcome.
- **Decisions** — bulleted list of what was decided.
- **Action items** — `owner: task (due date)` — one per line. Mark unowned/undated items clearly.
- **Open questions** — unresolved topics needing follow-up.

## Rules

- Don't invent owners or dates; mark them "unassigned" / "no date" if absent.
- Keep it scannable — short bullets, no paragraphs.
- Offer to store key decisions with `memory_store`.
