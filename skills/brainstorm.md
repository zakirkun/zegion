# Brainstorming & Ideation

Use this when the user wants ideas, options, or creative exploration.

## Procedure

1. Clarify constraints (budget, time, tools, audience) before generating.
2. Generate broadly first (10+ ideas), then converge.
3. Group ideas into 2-4 themes.
4. Recommend the strongest 1-3 with reasoning.

## Techniques

- Quantity first: don't self-censor during generation; evaluation comes after.
- Combine and remix: most good ideas are recombinations.
- Invert: ask "what would make this fail?" to find weaknesses.

## Output

- A themed list of ideas (terse, one line each).
- A short "top picks" section with pros/cons.
- A suggested next step to validate the best idea cheaply.
